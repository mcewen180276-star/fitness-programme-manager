Progression decision guide

Progression is a trainer-reviewed suggestion, not an automatic increase in weights, repetitions or duration.

  • Consider a small change only when the client completes the current work with consistent technique and reports manageable effort and good recovery.
  • Change one variable at a time, then review the response.
  • Maintain or reduce demands when technique changes, recovery is poor, confidence drops or the client reports concerns.
  • Stop and follow appropriate referral or screening procedures when new symptoms or health concerns arise.

Fitness calculations & assessment

Adult-only planning estimates. These are not diagnostic measurements or exercise clearance. Select and save a client to use their age, height, mass and resting heart rate.

Heart rate training zones

Estimated maximum heart rate = 208 − (0.7 × age). Heart-rate reserve uses resting heart rate and the Karvonen method. Predictions vary considerably by individual; medications and medical conditions can affect heart-rate response.

Body measurements & resting energy

BMI is a broad adult population screening measure, not a measure of fitness or body composition. BMR is an approximate resting energy expenditure, not a recommended food intake or calorie target.

Body fat measurement (optional)

A reliable body-fat percentage cannot be calculated from BMI alone. Enter an independently measured value if available, along with its measurement method; results from different devices/methods are not interchangeable.

Estimated one-repetition maximum (1RM)

Epley equation: load × (1 + reps / 30). Best used as an approximate reference for technically sound sets of 2–10 repetitions. Do not use it to prescribe maximal attempts.

Additional useful trainer metrics

Useful to track alongside these estimates: session RPE, training consistency, resting heart rate trends, and the actual loads/reps logged in Training Record. A single measurement should not dictate programme changes.

Client challenges

Set a goal and deadline, record the client's current level and available training time, then create an editable progression plan. The smart generator runs locally; it does not contact an AI service or provide medical clearance.

Generation requires an adult client with completed readiness screening and no unanswered concerns. Plans are drafts for trainer review, not guaranteed outcomes. A deadline may be unrealistic and should be adjusted based on progress and recovery.

Online exercise research

The generator first uses the client's screening, experience, availability and equipment. Optionally, search the public wger exercise database for exercises relevant to this challenge. Search results are reviewed before any exercise is imported or substituted. The generator itself is rules-based, not a live AI model.

Client Companion — free mini app

Export a standalone HTML mini app for the selected client. It includes their current training programme, challenge plans, and links to exercise technique demonstrations. They can log workouts and milestones offline, then send a results JSON file back to you.

Testing only: these files are not encrypted or password protected. Do not send real medical assessments or sensitive client details. Only a display name, workouts and challenge goals are included.
